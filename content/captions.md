# Launch grid captions

Nine posts, three per pillar. Post order below is publishing order: it opens
with the argument, moves to the model, then invites. Voice rules from
BRAND.md apply (no em-dashes, plain, not salesy).

Render graphics with:

    python3 content/build_posts.py
    ./content/render.sh posts/<slug> 1080 1080 content/out/<slug>.png

## Pillar 1 · The shift

### 01 — `01-plan-room.png`

AI is very good now at the things that used to sit at the centre of a fitness product: the personalised plan, the tracking, the answer at 6am. That's genuinely useful and it's here to stay.

So the honest question is what still holds value once information and customization are basically free.

The human part. Real guidance, accountability, and a group worth showing up for.

### 02 — `02-more-noise.png`

Fitness moved online and we ended up with more of everything. More apps, more programs, more videos, more information than anyone can actually use.

And the fight for attention turned into another quick hack, another secret you supposedly haven't heard yet.

Information was never the missing piece.

### 03 — `03-person.png`

The plan is rarely what people struggle with. Showing up is.

What holds value is the person, the accountability, and a room full of people going through the same thing at the same time.

## Pillar 2 · The model

### 04 — `04-two-build.png`

Your clients want complete guidance, and you are one specialist. That is not a problem to fix.

Complementary experts can build something neither could alone, each going all in on their own craft, and share the work as well as the revenue.

That's what INFITRA is for.

### 05 — `05-pairing.png`

A strength coach knows training. A nutritionist knows fuelling. Separately, two good offers. Together, one experience people actually finish.

Four to six weeks, designed together in one workspace, run live with a group.

### 06 — `06-ninety.png`

No upfront cost. No subscription. No lock-in.

Experts keep 90% of every sale, split as they agree in a recorded agreement. Your audience and your clients stay entirely yours, during and after.

### 07 — `07-tribe.png`

A video library doesn't build a community. A room does.
